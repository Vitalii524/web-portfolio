WEB PLATFORM

BeautySpace

A beauty industry web platform that brings clients, professionals, and salons together in one service.

UI / UX Frontend Web Platform Online Booking CRM

ABOUT THE PROJECT

One Service for Clients, Professionals, and Salons

BeautySpace is being developed as a full-featured web platform for the beauty industry.

Users can find a professional or salon, view services, prices, portfolios, and available times, then book online.

Professionals and salons get their own profiles and tools to manage services, schedules, bookings, clients, and portfolios.

ABOUT THE PROJECT

An Automotive Web Platform for Drivers and Businesses

Royal Garage is being developed as an automotive web platform for drivers, vehicle sellers, and members of the automotive community.

Users can add their vehicles, maintain service history, use the marketplace and forum, and communicate through the messaging system.

The platform also includes user profiles, a partner section, and tools for managing vehicle listings.
